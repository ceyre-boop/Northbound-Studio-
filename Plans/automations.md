# Northbound Automations: the first 10

Chosen with Colin on 2026-11-21. **Built for home services first** (roofers, plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, landscapers): high-value jobs, phone-heavy, lots of quotes, weak tech. Other businesses come later.

Every automation ships as a desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux) unlocked by a license key, except where marked **cloud**.

| # | Automation | Fixes | Runs | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Post everywhere** | Posting takes forever | App | Already built (Colin uses it himself). Sells when ready. **Counts as one of the 10.** |
| 1 | **Inbox autopilot** | Drowning in email | App | **Build first.** Based on the TABOOST inbox tool |
| 2 | **Lead catcher** | Missed leads | App | Instant reply and follow-ups for forms and DMs |
| 3 | **Quote builder** | Quotes take too long | App | Job description → priced quote in minutes |
| 4 | **Invoice chaser** | Chasing payments | App | Sends invoices and polite reminders until paid |
| 5 | **Appointment reminders** | No-shows | App | Text and email reminders with easy rescheduling |
| 6 | **Owner dashboard** | No idea what's working | App | Leads, sales and reviews in one weekly report |
| 7 | **Video maker** | Making content | App | Clips or ideas → ready-to-post videos (pairs with #10) |
| 8 | **Data entry** | Copying info between tools | App | Forms ↔ sheets ↔ CRM ↔ invoices |
| 9 | **AI receptionist** | Answering the phone | **Cloud** | Answers calls 24/7, takes messages, books jobs. The one cloud exception |

Pricing follows DECISIONS #24: $100–$300/mo by how niche. Broad ones (inbox, reminders, data entry) start near $100; niche ones (video maker, AI receptionist, quote builder) go toward $300.

Build order: #1 Inbox autopilot first, then one at a time. Each one goes live and gets used by a real client before the next starts.

## Settled details
- **Review getter** is not in the first 10. Revisit later.
- **Lead catcher, Invoice chaser and Appointment reminders stay apps.** They run while the client's computer is on (fine during the workday). Only the AI receptionist runs in the cloud.
- **Inbox autopilot v1** works with **Gmail and Outlook**.
- **Inbox autopilot starts in draft mode:** it drafts replies for the owner to send. Once the owner trusts it, they switch on auto-send.
